## Summary

13 of 14 ACs pass; 1 ACs ended in `error` due to an orchestrator-side retry-design issue (see AC 7 notes). The single `error` is **not** a code-under-test failure — the verify skill's runtime behavior matches what its own spec requires; the error came from how the verify *orchestrator* (this dogfood run) constructed its corrective re-dispatch prompt. The retry stripped the AC body and commit context, and the second-attempt subagent honestly returned `error` rather than fabricate.

This is a real, useful dogfood finding: **the verify skill's spec does not require retries to preserve full context.** The `subagent-verdict-contract` REQ says retries reiterate the verdict contract but is silent on whether the AC body and commit-list context must be re-included. In practice, subagents are isolated and need the full context every dispatch. Captured as sidekick seed for follow-up.
